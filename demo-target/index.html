SENTINEL / CONTROLLED FAILURE TARGET

A checkout built
to be safely broken.

This disposable app has protected health and synthetic checkout endpoints. It never takes payments or creates orders. Sentinel measures a nullable-promo regression, evaluates rollback safety, and verifies the recovery.

Routes: /api/health and /api/checkout. Both require the shared probe token. This page remains available during a checkout failure; page availability alone is not proof of recovery.
